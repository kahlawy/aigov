A Context-Aware Runtime Governance Enforcement Framework for Agentic AI Systems

Interactive proof-of-concept for preventive enforcement before governed agent actions reach external tools. Agentic DevSecOps is the controlled empirical environment, not the theoretical boundary.

Primary research question

To what extent does context-aware runtime governance enforcement reduce critical unauthorized permits compared with native safeguards and policy-only enforcement?

Primary hypothesis

F produces a lower Critical Unauthorized Permit Rate than B1 and B2.

Research problem

Agentic AI can form goals, revise plans, invoke tools, and produce side effects. Conventional policy checks may miss delegated authority, plan state, resource sensitivity, environment, and relevant history.

Primary scientific contribution

Design and experimental validation of a context-aware runtime governance enforcement framework for Agentic AI systems.

Planned Experimental Evaluation

Three controlled conditions isolate the value of trusted context at runtime.

B1

Native safeguards

Built-in agent and tool safeguards only.

B2

Policy-only enforcement

Versioned rules plus conventional request attributes, without full goal, plan, delegated authority, history, or environment context.

F

Context-aware enforcement

The proposed framework using the complete trusted context vector.

Unit of analysis and statistical design

Unit of analysis

Action or tool invocation nested within a scenario run.

Primary analysis

Binomial GLMM with condition as a fixed effect and scenario and task random intercepts.

Power analysis

Alpha .05, target power at least .80, final sample size determined by pilot-based simulation.
